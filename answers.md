## Q7. Which of these four promises does Rick Astley NOT make in the song?

- **Type:** Multiple choice (required)
- **Options:**
  1. Never gonna give you up
  2. Never gonna let you go
  3. Never gonna run around and desert you
  4. Never gonna say goodbye
- **Correct answer:** **2 — Never gonna let you go**
- **Why:**
  - **Options 1, 3, 4 are real promises from the song** — each is a complete chorus line found in the video's captions:
    - *Never gonna give you up*
    - *Never gonna run around and desert you*
    - *Never gonna say goodbye*
  - **Option 2 is the made-up distractor** — Rick never says "let you go" anywhere in the song. The song only ever says "never gonna **let you down**", which is easily misremembered as "let you go". That's the trap.
